Specialties

Cornea and refractive surgery

My own specialty since 2012: corneal disease and the correction of refractive error.

Spectacle removal by LASIK, ICL and IPCL. I hold the Gold Medal for the highest number of IPCL surgeries performed in the North Region.

Cataract and phaco surgery

Phacoemulsification cataract surgery at Netram, and free of cost at the doorstep through my foundation.

Through the Foundation's Nayan Hans programme, screening and advanced phaco surgery reach patients where they live, free of cost.

Glaucoma

Diagnosis and management of glaucoma by Netram's panel of six super-specialist doctors.

Retinal disorders

Retinal disorders are assessed and treated at the centre as part of its super-speciality services.

Comprehensive eye examination

A full eye examination, your spectacle prescription, and the work-up before any surgery, under one roof.

Netram covers everything from a general eye examination to complex surgery: cataract, glaucoma, retinal disorders and corneal disease.

Children and refractive error

Through Project Roshini we have screened more than one lakh school children for refractive error.

Screening in Delhi, Haryana and Rajasthan, with around 28,000 pairs of spectacles given out.

My journey

Fourteen years, one vision

From King George's Medical University to a super-speciality centre in C.R. Park, a foundation in twenty-four states, and the TEDx stage.

2009

I completed my MS in Ophthalmology at King George's Medical University, Lucknow, and went on to my senior residency at Maulana Azad Medical College, Delhi.

2012

My cornea and refractive practice began as a specialist ophthalmologist at Max and Fortis, Gurgaon. I organised my first charitable eye and health camps the same year.

2013

I left Max and Fortis to found Netram, a super-speciality eye centre in a two-room basement at C.R. Park, and Netram Eye Foundation, an all-India society with one vision: Good Health for All.

50+

Netram grew to fill all three floors of the building, with over 50 people and a panel of six super-specialist doctors covering everything from a general examination to complex surgery.

24

The Foundation's camps reached twenty-four states, from Delhi and the NCR to Assam, Gujarat and Tamil Nadu: Project Roshini, Nayan Hans, Eye Mela and Mission 6/6.

2026

Forbes India named me among its Business Leaders Defining Modern Success, and I gave a TEDx talk, "The Power of Sight", on how vision shapes opportunities.
